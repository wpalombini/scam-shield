# Dataset attribution

## `scamwatch.jsonl`

© Commonwealth of Australia. Material from [Scamwatch](https://www.scamwatch.gov.au), run by the Australian Competition and Consumer Commission, used under [CC BY 3.0 AU](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/au/). Each record's `source_url` links to its original alert.

Changes made: personal details replaced with placeholders, links defanged, formatting artefacts removed, and some text transcribed from image descriptions. Each record's `notes` field says what changed. Labels and categories are this project's own.

This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by the ACCC or Scamwatch.

## `uci_sms.jsonl`

From the [SMS Spam Collection](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/228/sms+spam+collection) by Tiago Almeida and José Hidalgo, UCI Machine Learning Repository, [DOI 10.24432/C5CC84](https://doi.org/10.24432/C5CC84), used under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes made: 100 messages labelled ham were selected after screening out names, numbers, links and intimate, health or otherwise sensitive content. The text is unchanged. Categories and hard-negative flags are this project's own.

This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by the dataset's creators.
